# Waypoint

吴凡的个人网站。以下术语区分个人经历、实践及其记录形式。
Wilf Wu's personal website. These terms distinguish personal experiences, practice, and the forms used to record them.

## Language

**山野（Trails）**:
以吴凡的跑山经历与个人选择为核心的内容集合。
A collection centered on Wilf's trail-running experiences and personal choices.

**创造（Making）**:
吴凡借助 AI 做出的作品集合，涵盖实用工具和出于好奇、趣味的创作。
A collection of things Wilf makes with AI, including useful tools and creations driven by curiosity or play.

**作品（Work）**:
借助 AI 做出、有可展示结果的东西，可以是应用、交互页面、视觉实验或小玩具。
Something made with AI that has a result to show, such as an app, an interactive page, a visual experiment, or a small toy.

**越野经历（Trail experience）**:
吴凡在跑山中亲历的过程与个人选择，比赛成绩是其中的一部分。
Wilf's firsthand experiences and personal choices on the trails, with race results forming one part of those experiences.

**AI 实践（Building with AI）**:
借助 AI 将个人想法付诸尝试、实现或改进的活动。
Using AI to try, realize, or improve personal ideas.

**赛记（Race report）**:
为留存比赛经历而写下的个人回顾。
A personal account of a race, written to preserve the experience.
_Avoid_: 比赛记录（Race record）

**比赛记录（Race record）**:
一场比赛的事实条目，包括名称、日期和已记录的成绩等。
A factual entry for a race, including its name, date, and recorded result.
_Avoid_: 赛记（Race report）

**正常浏览（Normal browsing）**:
Waypoint 以明确导航和常规页面结构呈现内容的浏览方式。当前唯一的浏览方式。
Waypoint presents content through explicit navigation and conventional document structure. This is the current browsing experience.

**材质表面（Material surface）**:
以中性光照、细颗粒和空间层次表现触感的内容区域，正文不随装饰运动。
A content surface with neutral light, subtle grain and spatial depth; reading copy remains still.

## Retired direction

探索模式、探索邀请、彩蛋与发现记录属于曾被讨论和实现的数字山野方案。吴凡于 2026-10-02 明确取消该方案；这些术语不再描述当前网站功能。
Explore mode, its invitation, Easter eggs and discovery records belonged to the digital-landscape proposal. Wilf withdrew that direction on 2 October 2026; these terms no longer describe current site features.
